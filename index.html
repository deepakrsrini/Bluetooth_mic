Tips

  • Pair the speaker first in your phone's Bluetooth settings. VoiceCast plays through whatever audio output is active.
  • Point the speaker away from you and keep a few metres between the phone and the speaker. Distance is the best fix for feedback.
  • Expect a short delay. Bluetooth adds about 100–250 ms that no app can remove, so for speeches use Hold to talk.
  • If you hear a squeal, lower the speaker volume first. The howl killer handles what's left.
